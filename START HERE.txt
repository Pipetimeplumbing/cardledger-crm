CARDLEDGER — COMPLETE PACKAGE v2.0.0

This ZIP contains the program, owner/customer login, $5/month subscription code,
Shopify connector, receipts, spreadsheet, and Windows launch/update tools.
It is not a self-contained EXE. Python 3.12 or newer is required for local use.

WINDOWS
1. Extract the entire ZIP to a permanent folder, such as Documents\CardLedger.
2. If you already use CardLedger, stop it. Run Update CardLedger.bat from this
   NEW folder and select your OLD installation. This preserves and backs up data.
   Continue using your original installation after the update.
3. For a new installation, open Open CardLedger.vbs once. It installs dependencies
   and opens the login page. Internet is required for first installation.
4. Run Setup Owner.bat once to choose your email and password. Then refresh the
   login page and sign in. Never share your password in chat or GitHub.
5. Use Open CardLedger.vbs to open the app without a command window.
   Use Stop CardLedger.vbs before updates or to stop the background program.
   If Windows blocks VBS files, use Start CardLedger.bat instead.

HOST ONLINE
Render connects to https://github.com/Pipetimeplumbing/cardledger-crm and pulls
the source automatically. It does not deploy by uploading this ZIP directly.
See HOSTING.md for the exact service, persistent disk, login, and Stripe settings.

Stripe billing, Shopify syncing, and public payment links need your own secure
configuration. They are not activated simply by downloading this package.
New customer signups are disabled unless you enable them on your host.

YOUR RECORDS
No existing inventory, customer records, or credentials are included in this ZIP.
Your live records are in your existing data folder. Keep it and back it up.
For hosting, transfer your data securely; do not put it on GitHub.

GOOGLE SHEETS
Upload spreadsheet\CardLedger-Google-Sheets.xlsx to Google Drive and open with
Google Sheets. The spreadsheet is separate from the app database, not a live sync.

README.md has full usage details. HOSTING.md covers subscriptions and hosting.
SHOPIFY.md covers Shopify connection and sync limitations.
